# 1. Purpose

This document defines the target enterprise architecture state of DairyOS.

It establishes the intended future architecture after completion of all planned capability evolution including:

- Complete operational management
- Enterprise farm data platform
- Cross-domain intelligence
- Predictive analytics
- Decision automation
- Knowledge management
- Autonomous operational assistance

This document provides the architectural direction for all future DairyOS development.

---

# 2. Target Architecture Vision

DairyOS will evolve into an enterprise-grade Dairy Operating System capable of managing the complete lifecycle of a dairy enterprise.

The target platform shall provide:

- Farm operational command and control
- Animal lifecycle intelligence
- Production optimization
- Financial management
- Resource planning
- Risk monitoring
- Executive decision support
- Predictive intelligence
- Knowledge-driven recommendations
- Controlled autonomous assistance

The target state transforms DairyOS from an operational software platform into an intelligent farm operating system.

# 8. AI Governance Boundary

Artificial Intelligence capabilities within DairyOS shall operate under strict governance.

AI shall:

- Support human decisions
- Provide explainable recommendations
- Maintain audit records
- Respect operational authority boundaries

AI shall not:

- Replace farm ownership decisions
- Execute uncontrolled actions
- Modify critical records without authorization

Human accountability remains the final authority.
