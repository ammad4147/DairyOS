# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Architecture Governance Framework for DairyOS.

It establishes the governance structure, decision-making process, responsibilities, controls, and compliance approach required to ensure DairyOS evolves in a controlled and sustainable manner.

Architecture governance ensures that all DairyOS development remains aligned with business capability, operational requirements, and enterprise architecture principles.

---

# 2. Architecture Governance Vision

DairyOS architecture governance provides:

- Controlled architectural evolution
- Consistent design decisions
- Protection of architectural integrity
- Alignment between business and technology
- Long-term sustainability

The governance objective is:

**Ensure every DairyOS capability evolves through disciplined architectural decision-making.**

---

# 3. Governance Principles

## 3.1 Architecture First Principle

Major system decisions shall consider architectural impact before implementation.

---

## 3.2 Controlled Evolution Principle

DairyOS shall evolve through planned and governed changes.

---

## 3.3 Ownership Principle

Every architectural capability shall have clear ownership responsibility.

---

## 3.4 Transparency Principle

Architecture decisions shall be documented and traceable.
