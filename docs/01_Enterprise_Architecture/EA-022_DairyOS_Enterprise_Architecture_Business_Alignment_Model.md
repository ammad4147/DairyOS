# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Business Alignment Model for DairyOS Enterprise Architecture.

It establishes the relationship between business objectives, dairy operational capabilities, enterprise architecture, technology decisions, and future system evolution.

The purpose is to ensure DairyOS technology development continuously supports real operational and business outcomes.

---

# 2. Business Alignment Architecture Vision

DairyOS enterprise architecture shall remain aligned with:

- Dairy farm operational objectives
- Management decision requirements
- Financial sustainability goals
- Animal productivity improvement
- Long-term business growth

The alignment objective is:

**Ensure every DairyOS capability delivers measurable operational and business value.**

---

# 3. Business Alignment Principles

## 3.1 Business Value First Principle

Architecture decisions shall prioritize business outcomes over technology complexity.

---

## 3.2 Operational Reality Principle

DairyOS capabilities shall reflect actual farm workflows and operational needs.

---

## 3.3 Decision Support Principle

System capabilities shall improve management visibility and decision quality.

---

## 3.4 Sustainable Growth Principle

Architecture shall support gradual expansion without unnecessary complexity.
