# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Enterprise Architecture Roadmap for DairyOS.

It establishes the planned architectural evolution path from the current operational foundation toward a complete intelligent dairy operating ecosystem.

The roadmap provides strategic direction while maintaining controlled and sustainable growth.

---

# 2. Architecture Roadmap Vision

DairyOS will evolve through structured maturity stages:

- Operational foundation
- Integrated operating platform
- Intelligence-enabled system
- Autonomous decision support ecosystem

The roadmap objective is:

**Transform DairyOS from a farm management platform into a complete dairy operating intelligence system.**

---

# 3. Roadmap Principles

## 3.1 Capability First Evolution

Architecture evolution shall follow business capability requirements.

---

## 3.2 Foundation Before Intelligence

Core operational capabilities shall be completed before advanced AI and predictive capabilities are introduced.

---

## 3.3 Controlled Maturity Growth

Each maturity stage shall provide measurable value before progressing further.

---

## 3.4 Sustainable Architecture Evolution

Future capability expansion shall preserve:

- Simplicity
- Maintainability
- Governance
- Operational reliability

---

# 4. DairyOS Architecture Maturity Model

## Stage 1 — Operational Foundation

Objective:

Establish the core DairyOS operating platform.

Capabilities:

- Farm operations
- Herd management
- Animal records
- Production tracking
- Core financial visibility

---

## Stage 2 — Integrated Dairy Operating System

Objective:

Connect operational capabilities into a unified platform.

Capabilities:

- Cross-domain workflows
- Integrated data model
- Executive dashboards
- Operational intelligence

---

## Stage 3 — Intelligence Platform

Objective:

Introduce advanced analytical capabilities.

Capabilities:

- Predictive analytics
- Decision support
- Risk identification
- Performance optimization

---

## Stage 4 — Autonomous Dairy Ecosystem

Objective:

Enable intelligent automation.

Capabilities:

- IoT integration
- Automated monitoring
- Autonomous recommendations
- Advanced AI assistance
