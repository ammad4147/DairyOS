1. Purpose and operating principles

DairyOS is a farm operating system. Enter a real-world fact once at its authoritative operational point; related modules reuse that persisted fact rather than asking for a second competing entry. Current primary navigation is Dashboard | Animals | Milk | Feed | Finance | Breeding | Health | Vaccination | COP. Settings and Audit are header controls; Payroll is a Finance window; DairyOS Administration is a separate privileged tool.

  • Permanent Animal ID is the identity anchor and remains traceable after sale or mortality.
  • Dates, sessions, quantities, categories and settlement state must reflect the actual event.
  • Feed purchases originate in Finance; Feed consumes the purchase/stock authority.
  • Finance is monetary authority for revenue, expenses, receivables, payables and OPEX.
  • COP/L = Feed Cost/L + OPEX/L. Automatic COP uses governed milk litres as denominator.
  • Warnings are resolved through their lifecycle; do not hide them by deleting the underlying fact.
  • Backup, restore, rollback, reset and purge belong to authenticated DairyOS Administration.

3. How data moves through DairyOS

  1. Operator selects the correct entry point.
  2. DairyOS validates required fields, eligibility, dates, category and quantities.
  3. The backend persists the authoritative record in PostgreSQL.
  4. Domain services update related state/ledgers.
  5. Dashboard, Passport, registers and analytics read that same authority.
  6. Governed conditions can raise findings/warnings.
  7. Corrections use supported lifecycle/void/audit actions instead of silently rewriting history.

4. Animals - master herd and Passport

Register Animal creates the permanent identity used everywhere. Important fields include Animal ID/identification, sex, DOB, breed, lifecycle/category, milking frequency, dam/sire, location and production group. Lifecycle/category drives herd totals and eligibility; milking frequency drives expected Milk sessions; lineage appears in breeding/Passport context.

Animals can be searched by ID, tag, RFID, breed or status. Sold/mortality records remain visible. The Passport consolidates animal-linked milk/lactation, breeding/lineage, health/treatment and lifecycle history; it is not a second competing ledger.

5. Milk - production, disposition and reconciliation

Enter Milk Production

Select the operational date, Animal ID and governed session and enter actual litres. DairyOS enforces the expected session sequence/frequency. Persisted session yields roll into animal/day and farm totals, production trends, Passport history, average yield, COP denominator and yield-drop monitoring.

Disposition

Account for produced milk using the real disposition: sale, domestic use, calf feed, wastage or other governed disposition. Sale links quantity/value and settlement to Finance. Reconciliation compares produced litres with accounted litres; unaccounted or over-accounted milk is a signal to correct the source event, not invent balancing data.

Yield Drop Watchlist

Animal-specific production declines create governed findings. Open the animal/comparison, investigate feed/health/management causes, and resolve the warning only when the operational condition is addressed.

6. Feed - stock, TMR and equipment

Feed purchases are entered in Finance > Record Expense > Feed Expenses. Feed then displays Finance Purchased, TMR Consumed, Manual Override, Storage Balance and Finance Rate. TMR consumption reduces governed stock. Use Manual Storage Override only for a verified physical correction and record a reason. Finance-purchased feed equipment appears in Feed with purchase reference; operational status can be maintained without duplicating the purchase.

7. Finance - revenue, expenses, receivables, payables and payroll

Record Revenue

Revenue types include Milk Sales, Organic Manure/Dung and category-specific animal sales: Milking, Dry, Heifer, Female Calf, Male Calf and Bull. Animal sale selectors must show only eligible animals in the selected category. A selected Animal ID represents one head; saving the sale marks that animal SOLD, removes it from active herd operations and retains its history.

For credit, record the actual cash received. The unpaid balance remains receivable. Do not mark an unpaid sale as received merely to clear the form.

Record Expense

Record the correct master category, date, supplier, amount, payment method and payable state. Feed purchases additionally carry item/quantity/unit rate so Feed stock is updated. Semen purchases capture semen type, sire/bull code/name, breed, batch/lot, expiry, storage/source and quantity; purchased lots become available in Breeding.

OPEX and COP

Finance expenses attributable to the selected period provide the governed OPEX monetary authority. Automatic OPEX/L divides attributable OPEX by governed milk litres for the same period.

Payroll

Finance Payroll records employee, role, period, worked days, base pay, overtime, allowances, advances and deductions. DairyOS previews overtime, gross and net pay. Mark Paid only when settlement actually occurs.

8. Breeding - AI, pregnancy, loss and calving

Eligible Milking, Dry and Heifer animals can receive governed breeding events. Record the actual AI/insemination date and purchased semen lot. PD Positive confirms pregnancy; PD Negative returns/revises the animal toward open status. Record miscarriage/abortion as the actual loss event. Calving closes gestation and records birth outcome/calf sex. Gestation age is measured from insemination, not PD confirmation. Analytics are derived from persisted cycles.

9. Health - cases, treatment and withdrawal

Record the Animal ID, clinical date, diagnosis/signs, treatment/medicine, severity, withdrawal, next check-up and veterinarian/notes. These records drive active sick lists, follow-up due/overdue, withdrawal state, Passport and health analytics. Mark Healthy only when genuinely resolved; do not bypass withdrawal obligations by closing a case early.

10. Vaccination - preventive protection

Use the action queue for due/overdue work. Record Animal ID, purpose/vaccine, given date, dose/route, batch and administrator. Persisted records drive GIVEN history, next-due state, overdue/due-next-30-days, vaccinated-this-month and animals-with-no-history projections.

11. COP - Cost of Production per Litre

Formula: COP/L = Feed Cost/L + OPEX/L.

Automatic: Feed Cost/L = governed feed cost / governed milk litres; OPEX/L = attributable Finance OPEX / governed milk litres. DairyOS must not fabricate a per-litre result without a governed milk denominator.

Manual: operator enters only Feed Cost/L and OPEX/L; DairyOS calculates their sum. Total milk is not a fourth manual component. An Estimated or Operator-Assessed result can be made official for the applicable month; the Dashboard then uses the persisted official value.

12. Dashboard - reading the command picture

The Dashboard is a projection/navigation surface, not a second source of truth. Milk cards come from Milk; herd totals from Animals; clinical/vaccination/reproductive cards from their modules; receivables from Finance; COP from official COP; warnings from the findings/audit lifecycle. If a Dashboard value looks wrong, trace it to its authoritative module and correct the real source record.

13. Audit Register and active warnings

The header bell shows unresolved warnings. The Audit Register shows warning details, Animal ID where applicable, created timestamp and lifecycle history. Resolve only after the condition is addressed and provide a meaningful note. Admin Reinstate reopens a resolved warning with an explicit reason and immutable lifecycle trail.

14. DairyOS Administration - password and recovery

DairyOS Administration is a separate privileged executable. First launch requires creation of an Admin password and issues a recovery key that must be stored securely outside the computer. Future sessions require the password. Forgot Password uses the current recovery key; successful recovery/change rotates the key and invalidates the previous one.

Capabilities: validate installation/database, create verified backup, restore verified backup, rollback, reset application data, permanent purge, uninstall while keeping data, change/recover password and review administrative audit history. Restore, rollback, reset, purge and uninstall require fresh password re-entry; reset/purge retain operation-specific confirmation and recovery safeguards.

15. Installation, reinstallation and uninstallation

When existing DairyOS data is detected, the installer must not silently replace it. The operator can reuse existing data or route verified restoration through authenticated DairyOS Administration. On normal uninstall, farm data/private PostgreSQL are retained by default. The operator can continue while keeping data or cancel to create a verified backup first. Permanent deletion remains Admin-only.

16. How to use DairyOS - daily operating routine

Start of day

  1. Review Dashboard and Active Warnings.
  2. Check herd counts for plausibility.
  3. Review Health follow-ups, Vaccination due/overdue work and Breeding due events.

Milking

  1. Milk > Enter Milk Production.
  2. Select correct date, Animal ID and governed session.
  3. Enter actual litres; repeat for each animal/session.
  4. Review farm total, missing sessions and Yield Drop Watchlist.

Feed/stores

  1. Enter purchases in Finance.
  2. Monitor Feed stock/TMR consumption.
  3. Use Manual Override only after verified physical reconciliation.

Milk leaving/used

  1. Record the actual disposition.
  2. For sale, record buyer, litres, rate and actual cash received.
  3. Review reconciliation; correct unexplained or over-accounted milk.

Money

  1. Use correct Finance revenue/expense type and date.
  2. Preserve genuine receivable/payable balances.
  3. For animal sale, verify category and exact Animal ID before saving.
  4. For feed/semen purchases, complete the operational detail needed by Feed/Breeding.

Breeding

  1. Record actual AI and semen lot.
  2. Record actual PD result.
  3. Record pregnancy loss/calving when they occur.

Health and vaccination

  1. Record clinical treatment promptly and observe withdrawal/follow-up.
  2. Mark healthy only after recovery.
  3. Record vaccinations from the preventive action queue with batch/administrator details.

End of day

  1. Confirm expected milk sessions.
  2. Review milk reconciliation.
  3. Review Feed/TMR and Finance transactions.
  4. Review Health/Vaccination/Breeding obligations and warnings.
  5. Use Dashboard as a cross-module reasonableness check.
  6. Create verified backups at the farm-defined cadence and before upgrades or major corrections.

17. Corrections and safe operating practice

  • Never create fake balancing entries.
  • Never delete an animal merely because it was sold or died.
  • Do not edit Dashboard projections; correct the authoritative source.
  • Do not use Feed override to represent a Finance purchase.
  • Do not mark credit as cash received.
  • Do not create a new breeding event to hide a negative PD/loss.
  • Do not close Health while obligations remain.
  • Do not reset/purge to fix ordinary entry mistakes.
  • Before restore/reset/purge/upgrade, create and verify a backup.
