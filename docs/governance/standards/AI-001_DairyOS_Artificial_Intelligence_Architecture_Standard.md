# AI-001

# DairyOS Artificial Intelligence Architecture Standard


## 1. Purpose

AI-001 establishes the architecture principles and governance framework for Artificial Intelligence capabilities within DairyOS.


## 2. Strategic Intent

DairyOS AI capabilities transform governed operational data into actionable intelligence.


AI supports:

- better decisions
- early risk identification
- operational optimisation
- predictive capability


## 3. Governing Principle

AI shall augment human decision-making, not replace operational responsibility.


## 4. Scope

Applies to:

- machine learning models
- predictive analytics
- recommendation engines
- optimisation systems
- AI assistants
- future autonomous intelligence capabilities


## 5. AI Architecture Principles

DairyOS AI follows:

- operational first
- data trust
- explainability
- human authority
- controlled evolution


## 6. DairyOS Intelligence Architecture Model


Decision Intelligence Layer

↓

Predictive Intelligence Layer

↓

Analytical Intelligence Layer

↓

Operational Intelligence Layer

↓

Governed DairyOS Data Platform

↓

Operational Domains


## 7. AI Capability Layers


### Layer 1 — Operational Intelligence

Supports:

- alerts
- reminders
- exception detection
- workflow assistance


### Layer 2 — Analytical Intelligence

Supports:

- trends
- cost analysis
- performance analysis
- benchmarking


### Layer 3 — Predictive Intelligence

Supports:

- disease risk prediction
- production forecasting
- breeding probability
- financial forecasting


### Layer 4 — Decision Intelligence

Supports:

- recommendations
- scenario analysis
- optimisation
- strategic planning


## 8. AI Domain Relationship

AI consumes information from:

- Herd Domain
- Health Domain
- Production Domain
- Feed Domain
- Breeding Domain
- Finance Domain
- Operations Domain


AI does not own operational truth.


## 9. AI Data Requirements

AI datasets require:

- ownership
- validation
- historical completeness
- documented meaning


## 10. Machine Learning Governance

Models require:

- documented purpose
- input definitions
- output definitions
- performance measurements
- review history


## 11. AI Model Lifecycle


Problem Definition

↓

Data Preparation

↓

Model Development

↓

Validation

↓

Approval

↓

Deployment

↓

Monitoring

↓

Retirement


## 12. AI Recommendation Standards

AI outputs shall include:

- recommendation
- supporting information
- confidence indicator
- affected domain


## 13. AI Safety Boundaries

AI shall not:

- change critical records without approval
- modify financial transactions autonomously
- override veterinary decisions
- bypass security controls


## 14. AI Integration Architecture

AI services communicate through:

- approved APIs
- controlled data access
- documented interfaces


## 15. AI Security Requirements

AI systems shall protect:

- operational data
- business information
- model assets


Requirements:

- authentication
- authorization
- audit logging


## 16. AI Monitoring

AI systems shall monitor:

- accuracy
- reliability
- data quality
- model drift


## 17. Future AI Capabilities

Potential capabilities:

- digital dairy twin
- predictive herd management
- autonomous reporting
- optimisation engines


## 18. Relationship With DATA-001

DATA-001 provides trusted information.

AI-001 defines intelligent usage.


## 19. Relationship With API-001

API-001 defines communication.

AI-001 defines AI service behaviour.


## 20. Relationship With DOM-001

DOM-001 defines business ownership.

AI supports those capabilities.
