# 1. Purpose

Defines the operating model used by DairyOS to represent dairy farm activities.

---

# 2. Farm Operating Model

The farm operates through:

## Animal Lifecycle Management

Includes:

- Birth
- Growth
- Breeding
- Lactation
- Dry period
- Replacement

---

## Daily Operations

Includes:

- Feeding
- Milking
- Cleaning
- Observation
- Task execution

---

## Management Operations

Includes:

- Planning
- Monitoring
- Corrective action
- Performance review

---

# 3. Operating Model Principles

- Standardized workflows
- Clear ownership
- Timely information
- Operational accountability
