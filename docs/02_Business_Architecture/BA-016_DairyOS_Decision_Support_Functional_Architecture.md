# BA-016 — DairyOS Decision Support Functional Architecture

**Document ID:** BA-016
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved Baseline

---

# 1. Purpose

Defines the decision support capability architecture for DairyOS.

---

# 2. Decision Support Vision

DairyOS shall transform operational information into actionable management insight.

---

# 3. Functional Capabilities

## Operational Intelligence

Supports:

- Status monitoring
- Exception identification

---

## Decision Assistance

Supports:

- Recommendations
- Priority identification

---

## Management Intelligence

Supports:

- Performance analysis
- Future optimization

---

# 4. Intelligence Evolution Boundary

Decision support shall enhance human decisions and remain aligned with governance principles.
