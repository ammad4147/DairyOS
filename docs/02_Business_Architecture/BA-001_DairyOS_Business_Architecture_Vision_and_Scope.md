# BA-001 — DairyOS Business Architecture Vision and Scope

**Document ID:** BA-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved Baseline

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the business architecture vision and scope for DairyOS.

DairyOS is designed as an operational management platform for modern dairy farm management.

---

# 2. Business Architecture Vision

DairyOS shall provide a structured operating environment connecting:

- Farm operations
- Animal management
- Production
- Finance
- Decision support

The objective is:

**Create a complete digital operating system for dairy farm management.**

---

# 3. Business Scope

DairyOS covers:

- Herd lifecycle management
- Animal health
- Reproduction
- Nutrition
- Milk production
- Inventory
- Financial management
- Executive oversight

---

# 4. Business Architecture Principles

- Operations first
- Data-driven management
- Practical farm workflows
- Controlled automation
- Sustainable expansion
