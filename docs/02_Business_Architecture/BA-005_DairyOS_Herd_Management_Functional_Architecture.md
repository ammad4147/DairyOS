# BA-005 — DairyOS Herd Management Functional Architecture

**Document ID:** BA-005
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved Baseline

---

# 1. Purpose

Defines the functional architecture for DairyOS Herd Management capability.

---

# 2. Herd Management Vision

DairyOS shall maintain complete lifecycle visibility of every animal from entry into the herd until removal.

---

# 3. Functional Capabilities

## Animal Identity Management

Supports:

- Unique animal identification
- Breed information
- Parentage records
- Ownership status

---

## Lifecycle Management

Supports:

- Calf stage
- Heifer stage
- Lactation stage
- Dry period
- Culling stage

---

## Herd Performance Management

Supports:

- Production history
- Reproductive history
- Health history
- Replacement planning

---

# 4. Business Rules

Herd decisions shall be based on:

- Animal records
- Performance data
- Economic value
