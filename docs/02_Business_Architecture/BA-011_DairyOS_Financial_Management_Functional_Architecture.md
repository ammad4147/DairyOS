# BA-011 — DairyOS Financial Management Functional Architecture

**Document ID:** BA-011
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved Baseline

---

# 1. Purpose

Defines the financial management capability architecture for DairyOS.

---

# 2. Financial Management Vision

DairyOS shall provide financial visibility connecting operational activities with business performance.

---

# 3. Functional Capabilities

## Cost Management

Supports:

- Feed costs
- Labour costs
- Veterinary costs
- Operational expenses

---

## Revenue Management

Supports:

- Milk revenue
- Animal sales
- Other income

---

## Financial Performance

Supports:

- Profitability analysis
- Cost trends
- Decision support

---

# 4. Financial Governance

Financial information shall support responsible farm management decisions.
