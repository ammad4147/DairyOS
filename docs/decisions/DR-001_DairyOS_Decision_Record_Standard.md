# DR-001

# DairyOS Decision Record Standard


## 1. Purpose

DR-001 establishes the standard method for recording significant DairyOS decisions.


## 2. Strategic Intent

Decision records preserve:

- decision history
- reasoning
- alternatives considered
- consequences
- future understanding


## 3. Governing Principle

Decisions without records become forgotten assumptions.


## 4. Scope

Applies to decisions involving:

- enterprise architecture
- business capabilities
- domain boundaries
- data governance
- technology direction
- security
- operations


## 5. Decision Record Definition

A Decision Record captures:

- significant choice
- context
- reasoning
- expected impact


## 6. Decision Record Lifecycle


Decision Identified

↓

Analysis

↓

Options Evaluation

↓

Recommendation

↓

Approval

↓

Registration

↓

Implementation

↓

Review


## 7. When Required


A decision record is required when a decision:

- affects architecture
- changes governance
- creates long-term commitment
- impacts multiple domains
- introduces significant risk


## 8. Decision Record Structure


Required sections:

- Decision Identity
- Context
- Decision Statement
- Options Considered
- Decision Rationale
- Consequences
- Dependencies
- Review Requirements


## 9. Decision Categories


Architecture Decision

Business Decision

Data Decision

Governance Decision

Operational Decision


## 10. Decision Status Model


| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Proposed | Under consideration |
| Accepted | Approved decision |
| Rejected | Considered but declined |
| Superseded | Replaced by another decision |
| Archived | Historical record |


## 11. Relationship With DPL-001


DPL-001 provides decision tracking.

DR-001 provides detailed decision knowledge.


## 12. Relationship With EKG-001


Decision records become enterprise knowledge relationships.


## 13. Approval Governance


Approval requires:

- owner identification
- impact assessment
- rationale
- approval authority


## 14. AI Governance


AI may assist:

- discovery
- summarization
- conflict identification


AI shall not:

- create authoritative decisions
- approve decisions
- remove history


## 15. Decision Quality Rules


Rule 1:

Major decisions require context.


Rule 2:

Alternatives should be considered.


Rule 3:

Rejected options should be recorded.


Rule 4:

Consequences must be understood.


Rule 5:

Decisions remain traceable.


## 16. Long-Term Vision


DR-001 establishes DairyOS as a knowledge-preserving enterprise.
