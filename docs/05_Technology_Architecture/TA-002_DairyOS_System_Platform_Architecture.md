**Document ID:** TA-002

# 1. Purpose

Defines the DairyOS platform architecture.

---

# 2. Platform Architecture Vision

DairyOS shall operate as a modular enterprise platform supporting independent business capabilities.

---

# 3. Platform Layers

## User Layer

Provides:

- Dashboards
- Interfaces
- Reports

---

## Application Layer

Provides:

- Business modules
- Services
- Workflows

---

## Data Layer

Provides:

- Storage
- Persistence
- Data management

---

## Intelligence Layer

Provides:

- Analytics
- Decision support

---

# 4. Platform Evolution

The platform shall support future AI and advanced analytics expansion.
