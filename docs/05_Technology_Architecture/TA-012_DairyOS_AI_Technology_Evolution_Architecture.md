# TA-012 — DairyOS AI Technology Evolution Architecture

**Document ID:** TA-012
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved Baseline

---

# 1. Purpose

Defines the future AI technology evolution direction.

---

# 2. AI Technology Vision

DairyOS shall evolve toward intelligent decision support while maintaining operational system integrity.

---

# 3. AI Technology Foundation

Supports:

- Data readiness
- Analytics capability
- Machine learning expansion

---

# 4. AI Evolution Principles

- Human decision support
- Responsible AI use
- Data quality first
- Governance controlled

---

# 5. AI Boundary

AI capabilities shall enhance DairyOS operations and shall not replace controlled business processes.

---
